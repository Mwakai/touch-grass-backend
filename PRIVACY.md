# Touch Grass privacy policy

Version 2026-10-28

This policy is written for parents and guardians. Touch Grass is an app that gets kids outside with challenges they complete and you approve. We collect as little as we can about your child, and you stay in control of it.

**Who we are:** [Company or founder name], [postal address]. Contact: [privacy email address].

## What we collect

**About you (the parent):**

- Your email address and a password (we store the password scrambled, so nobody can read it).
- The date you agreed to this policy, and which version you agreed to.

**About your child, only what you or your child enter:**

- First name or nickname, age, an avatar colour and the interests they pick.
- A username and a 4-digit PIN that you choose for them (the PIN is stored scrambled).
- The challenges they complete, whether you approved them, when, and any short note they add (up to 280 characters).
- When you gave consent for them to use Touch Grass.

## What we never collect

- Your child's email address, phone number, or home address.
- Your child's location. The app doesn't use GPS and doesn't record where a challenge was done.
- Photos of your child. If a challenge suggests taking a photo, it stays on your device and is never uploaded to us.
- We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

## Who can see your child's information

- You, from your parent account.
- Your child, from their own login.
- [Our hosting provider, MongoDB Atlas, stores the data in [region]. List any other service that processes data, for example an email provider.]

Nobody else. Kids can't message each other or see other families.

## Your consent

When you create an account, and again when you add each child, you confirm that you are their parent or guardian and agree to this policy. Kids can't create their own accounts.

## Deleting data

- **One child:** delete them from your parent account. Their profile, login and challenge history are removed straight away.
- **Everything:** delete your account. You, all your kids' profiles, logins and challenge history are removed straight away.

You can also email [privacy email address] to see, correct or delete your data. We reply within [30] days.
